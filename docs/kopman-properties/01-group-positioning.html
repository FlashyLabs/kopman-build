Most builders sell their margin. Kopman Group is being built to keep it — by owning the buildings its own trades improve, and running both on software it already wrote.

Sheet 01.1  —  The position

What Kopman Group is

Kopman Group is a Greater Toronto Area real estate and construction group. It has been building since 1999, across the GTA and Muskoka, under the Kopman Build name. The group structure formalises what the business already does — and adds the arm that has been missing.

The operating claim is narrow and testable: a group that can design, permit, build, own and operate a property internally does not pay anyone else to do those things. Every fee a conventional developer pays out — general contractor, project manager, construction manager — is a fee Kopman Group pays to itself, and can therefore convert into equity in the asset rather than expense against it.

That is the entire thesis. Everything below is its consequence.

Sheet 01.2  —  Group architecture

How the arms connect

The group is not a holding company with unrelated subsidiaries. Each arm is a supplier to, or a customer of, the others. The edges below are the point — they are what a competitor buying any single one of these businesses could not replicate.

Sheet 01.3  —  The arms

Five arms, three live

ARM 01

Kopman Build

kopmanbuild.com  ·  operating since 1999

The construction arm and the group's origin. Custom homes, full-house renovations, additions, laneway houses and design-build across the GTA and Muskoka. It carries the licences, the trade relationships, the estimating discipline and the twenty-six-year track record that every other arm borrows credibility from.

Role in the group: the production capability. It is the reason Kopman Properties can underwrite a retrofit at cost instead of at a quote.

ARM 02

Kopman Properties

kopman.properties  ·  launching

The properties and ownership arm. Its opening thesis is industrial: acquire GTA warehouses that are structurally sound but functionally obsolete — grade-level loading only, no docks, tired services — and retrofit them into space the ordinary shipping, storage and small-bay tenant can actually occupy. Detailed in Sheet 02.

Role in the group: the balance sheet. It converts Build's labour into owned, appreciating assets and recurring rent.

ARM 03

BuildOS

software  ·  built, pre-launch

The group's operating system, and already a real codebase rather than a plan: a multi-tenant platform covering CRM and lead capture, project delivery, quoting with an AI video-to-quote pipeline, inventory, referrals, loyalty, marketing automation, a trade marketplace, cost intelligence and white-label deployment — with billing and tenant onboarding wired.

Role in the group: the margin defence. It runs Build and Properties, and is licensable to the same builders Kopman competes with — turning operational discipline into a second revenue line.

ARM 04

Kopman Capital

planned  ·  not before the third asset closes

The co-investment vehicle. Once Kopman Properties has three completed retrofits with audited cost-to-complete and realised rent, the track record supports raising outside capital against it — deal-by-deal syndication first, a discretionary vehicle only later. Standing this arm up before there is a track record to sell would invert the sequence and price the group's credibility against a spreadsheet.

ARM 05

Kopman Muskoka

planned  ·  already latent in Build's demand

Recreational and cottage-country development. Kopman Build already markets to Muskoka and holds the operating knowledge — shoreline road allowances, seasonal trade scheduling, septic and well work — that GTA-only builders lack. The arm exists to stop that knowledge being sold one cottage at a time.

Sheet 01.4  —  The compounding logic

Where the same crew ends up

A construction business and a property business look similar from the outside — both put crews on sites. They are not similar. The difference is what the group owns when the crew drives away.

Cost basis, not quoted price

Retrofit scope enters the underwriting at Build's internal cost. A competing buyer underwrites the same building at a general contractor's tendered price. On identical assumptions, Kopman can bid higher and still clear its return — or bid the same and clear more.

Certainty of execution

The largest risk in value-add industrial is that the retrofit overruns. Kopman is not estimating a stranger's ability to deliver — it is scheduling its own crews, against its own historical cost data, on scope it performs weekly.

Two revenue shapes

Construction revenue is lumpy, won job-by-job, and stops when the phone stops. Rent is contracted, recurring and bankable. The group is deliberately building the second on top of the first.

Sheet 01.5  —  Domain & brand architecture

Three domains, one entity

The group runs separate domains rather than folding everything into one site. That is the right call — a warehouse investor and a homeowner planning a kitchen share no vocabulary, no buying cycle and no notion of what "good" looks like. But separate domains only work if search engines and language models can see that one organisation stands behind all three.

The rules that hold it together

  1. One entity graph. kopman.group declares both operating companies as subOrganization; each child declares parentOrganization back. Every domain carries the same sameAs set. This is what makes the three sites one entity to a search engine rather than three unrelated small businesses.
  2. Cross-links are editorial, never sitewide. A footer link farm between sibling domains is the pattern search engines discount. A link from a Properties case study to the Build page describing the exact retrofit trade is the pattern they reward. Detailed in Sheet 04.
  3. One visual system, two temperatures. Shared typography and near-black; Build stays warm linen, Properties moves to concrete. A visitor should recognise the family without mistaking the two businesses for each other. Detailed in Sheet 03.
  4. The group site never competes for the children's keywords. It ranks for Kopman Group, leadership names and corporate queries. It must not publish a page targeting "renovation contractor Toronto" — that cannibalises the arm that has earned it.
Sheet 01.6  —  Sequence

Order of operations

The sequence below is deliberate. Each phase exists to produce the evidence the next phase has to sell.

Phase 0
Immediate

Repair the foundation

Before either new domain launches, fix what the audit found on kopmanbuild.com: the contact form that discards every enquiry, the 574 unlinked pages, the placeholder offices and phone numbers, and the absence of any analytics. New domains inheriting this stack would inherit these faults. Non-negotiable and first — it is also the cheapest work in this document.

Phase 1
Q4 2026

Stand up the group entity

kopman.group launches as a small, dense corporate site — position, arms, leadership, contact. Entity schema wired across all three domains simultaneously so the graph is consistent from day one.

Phase 2
Q4 2026 – Q1 2027

First acquisition

Kopman Properties closes one dock-deficient GTA warehouse. kopman.properties launches alongside it — thesis, standards, and the live project documented from before-photos forward. One real building outranks any amount of claimed capability.

Phase 3
2027

Complete, lease, publish

Retrofit delivered, space leased, and the full cost-to-complete and rent achieved published as a case study. This document becomes the group's single most valuable marketing asset and the basis of every later conversation with capital.

Phase 4
2027 – 2028

Repeat twice, then raise

Assets two and three, on the same playbook, to prove the first was a process rather than a fluke. Only then does Kopman Capital open, and only against three audited outcomes.

Phase 5
2028+

Externalise BuildOS

The software is licensed to third-party builders once it has run three of the group's own retrofits end to end. Selling an operating system the seller does not run on is the fastest way to lose the room.

Sheet 01.7  —  Candour

What has to be true

The thesis is sound but not costless. These are the conditions that would break it, stated plainly so the board can price them.

Internal work must be priced honestly

"Build at cost" is an advantage only if the cost is real. If Build absorbs overruns on group projects to make Properties' returns look good, the group has moved a loss rather than avoided one. Every internal contract needs an arm's-length written scope and a market benchmark, or the reported returns are fiction — and any future outside investor will find it.

Construction capacity is finite and rivalrous

Every crew-week spent on a group asset is a crew-week not sold to a paying client. The first retrofit will compete directly with Build's revenue. That opportunity cost belongs in the underwriting as a real line, not a footnote.

Industrial is a different discipline, not an adjacent one

Residential construction expertise does not transfer cleanly to dock design, truck-court geometry, ESFR sprinkler engineering or industrial leasing. The group needs at least one person with genuine industrial experience before the first close — hired, partnered or retained. This is the largest single execution gap.

Environmental risk can end a deal after closing

Older GTA industrial buildings carry contamination history. A Phase II finding can exceed the entire retrofit budget. Every acquisition must be conditional on Phase I, and on Phase II wherever prior use warrants it — with no exceptions made for a competitive process.

Credibility is currently borrowed against claims not yet verified

kopmanbuild.com presently advertises offices in New York, London, Dubai and Singapore with "address forthcoming" and placeholder telephone numbers, alongside press mentions and project statistics its own documentation marks as illustrative. An institutional properties arm cannot launch behind that. Either substantiate the claims or remove them — before a broker or a capital partner reads them first.

Sheet 01.8  —  Position

The sentence

Kopman Group builds in the Greater Toronto Area and Muskoka, and increasingly builds for itself — buying buildings that need exactly the work it has done since 1999, and keeping what that work creates.

Every page across the three domains should be defensible as an expansion of that sentence. Anything that is not is either a different company or a claim the group has not yet earned.
