Entity hub,
not link ring.

The brief asked for a ring keyword cluster. The structure is right; the word is wrong, and the difference decides whether this works. Here is the architecture, the clusters per domain, and the one finding that should move most of the budget.

Sheet 04.1  —  The correction

Why "ring" is the wrong word

A literal three-way link ring between commonly-owned domains does not fail because it is penalised. It fails because it is silently discounted — search engines recognise same-entity links and give them little weight. No notice, no manual action, nothing to appealSource.

The pattern that does attract enforcement is narrower and easy to avoid. A legitimate corporate group trips several private-blog-network signals by nature — shared hosting, shared analytics, shared WHOIS, a shared design system. Those are not the violation. The signal that separates a corporate group from a link network is anchor text and placement. A group cross-links with brand anchors in identity contexts. A network cross-links with commercial exact-match anchors from body content.

One hard rule for the build

Never use a commercial-intent exact-match anchor on any cross-domain link between Kopman properties. Brand and navigational anchors only. "Kopman Build" and "part of the Kopman Group" are correct. "warehouse for sale Scarborough" pointing from one Kopman domain to another is the exact pattern that gets a network classified as a network.

What the ring is actually for

The payoff is not PageRank — that gets discounted regardless. It is entity consolidation: making "Kopman" resolve as one organisation across Google's Knowledge Graph and, more importantly, across language-model retrieval, so a mention of any arm reinforces all three. Design the ring for machine entity resolution, not for link equity. That changes what you build — schema, consistent identity data, shared press surfaces and a small number of genuinely editorial cross-links, not a templated footer block.

There is a real cost to running three domains, and the board should price it honestly: kopman.properties starts at zero authority and cannot borrow kopmanbuild.com's by linking to it. Separation is still the right call — a warehouse investor and a homeowner planning a kitchen share no vocabulary or buying cycle — but budget for Properties to earn its own authority rather than assuming it inherits any.

Cluster D — The intersection

the asset nobody else can build

Verified finding: there is no authoritative Ontario-specific content on the cost, permit path, or feasibility of adding loading docks to an existing industrial building. The search results for those queries return US equipment suppliers and dock-service vendors almost exclusively. The single Ontario result found was a concrete subcontractor's pageSource.

The best-performing Canadian article on industrial retrofits is a Globe and Mail piece from over a decade ago. That is how open this field is.

Why this cluster is the strategy
  1. Uncontested in Canada — verified empty, not assumed empty.
  2. It sits exactly at Build × Properties, which makes it the legitimate, non-manufactured justification for every sibling cross-link in Sheet 04.2.
  3. It is backed by proprietary cost data Kopman actually owns from real projects — the one thing that survives scaled-content enforcement and the one thing language models preferentially cite.
  4. It recruits vendors. The owner of a dock-deficient warehouse searching "cost to add a loading dock Ontario" is a potential seller. This cluster is an acquisition channel disguised as content marketing.
The vocabulary bridge — a cheap, real edge

Canadian brokers and tenants say "truck level" and "drive-in." US content — which dominates the corpora language models are trained on — says "dock-high" and "grade-level." Kopman must use both, consistently, on every page: the models are trained on US-majority text while the buyers type Canadian terms. Almost nobody bridges this deliberately.

Sheet 04.4  —  LLM strategy

The finding that should move the budget

A study of 75,000 brands measured which signals correlate with being surfaced in AI answersSource. The result reorders conventional SEO priorities substantially.

Mentions on YouTube 0.737 Branded web mentions 0.664 Branded anchor text 0.527 Brand search volume 0.392 Backlinks 0.218 Content volume 0.194

Correlation with AI visibility. Correlation is not causation, and these are relative rather than absolute — but the ordering is consistent across replications.

The strategic implication

Branded mentions correlate roughly three times as strongly as backlinks, and content volume ranks last. Earned media accounts for around 84% of AI citations; paid placement accounts for 0.3%Source. Content distributed through third-party outlets was cited at 34% against an 8% baseline for the brand's own siteSource.

The ring strategy as originally framed is an owned-media strategy. The highest-return investment available here is not the 700th page — it is getting "Kopman" said out loud in RENX, Daily Commercial News, Storeys, BILD and OHBA channels, trade press and on YouTube. The group structure helps: three brands give three story angles that all co-occur with the same name.

One targeting note with real teeth: there is only about 2% overlap between the journalists PR teams pitch most and the journalists AI actually citesSource. Chase the publications that appear in AI answers for your target queries, not the ones with the most prestige. Run the queries first, then build the media list from what you see.

Two things widely sold as AI strategy that the evidence does not support

Schema markup does not drive AI citations

A matched-control study of 1,885 pages that added JSON-LD, against ~4,000 controls, measured AI Mode +2.4%, ChatGPT +2.2% and AI Overviews −4.6% — statistical noiseSource. A separate controlled experiment found ChatGPT and Perplexity tokenise JSON-LD as raw text rather than parsing it as structured data.

Do not abandon schema — reclassify it. Its justification is Google rich results and Knowledge Graph entity resolution, where it genuinely works and where this group needs it (Sheet 04.6). It is a one-time infrastructure spend, not an ongoing AI programme. If a vendor pitches schema as a generative-search tactic, that pitch is behind the evidence.

llms.txt is not a strategy

Across 300,000 domains, adoption is about 10%. Of the roughly 38,000 domains with a valid file, 97% received zero requests for it in the month measured. Google's documentation states it has no effect on Search or AI Overviews — the file is simply ignoredSource.

Ship it if it costs nothing. Never put it on a strategy slide, and never pay for it. What actually matters at the crawler layer is the opposite action: verify that robots.txt does not block GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended or bingbot. Accidental AI-crawler blocking is common and would silently zero out this entire programme.

What does work, once you are in the candidate set

The peer-reviewed study on generative-engine optimisation (SIGKDD 2024, ~10,000 queries) tested content modifications and found overall visibility gains of 22–41%Source:

TacticReported liftApplication to Kopman
Add direct quotations~41%Quote the brokers, engineers and municipal staff involved in a retrofit
Add statistics~33%Publish real cost-per-dock and rent data — Kopman has this, nobody else does
Improve fluency~29%Editorial pass on everything published

Keyword stuffingnegative—

The limitation matters as much as the finding. The study tested rewriting a passage that had already been retrieved. It tells you how to win the citation once your page is in the candidate set; it says nothing about getting into that set. Sequence accordingly: get indexed → get mentioned → then optimise the passage. Secondary sources disagree on the exact per-tactic percentages — verify against the paper before quoting them externally.

Writing for retrieval

Answer in self-contained blocks

Google's AI Mode decomposes one query into 8–16 parallel sub-queries and selects passages, not pages. Write 40–170-word blocks that each fully resolve one sub-question without needing the surrounding context. A page that answers several sub-queries at once outranks one that answers only the head question.

Tables beat prose

Comparison matrices and tables present discrete labelled data points that models parse without ambiguity. A cost table with units and sources will be cited where the same information in paragraphs will not.

Length, then freshness

Long-form is cited around 3.2× more than sub-1,000-word content, with an optimum near 2,500–3,500 words. But 85% of AI Overview citations come from content published in the last two years. A refresh cadence tied to real data updates is required — bumping a timestamp without a substantive change is itself a scaled-content signal.

Submit to Bing

ChatGPT's retrieval draws on Bing's index alongside its own crawler. Bing Webmaster Tools registration for all three domains is free, takes an afternoon, and is skipped by most Canadian firms. It is the cheapest action in this document.

Sheet 04.5  —  Risk

Prune Build before expanding Properties

Google's March 2026 core update explicitly named scaled content abuse. Sites publishing large sets of near-identical pages saw traffic losses reported at 60–90%Source. The policy does not prohibit templated or AI-assisted content — it targets volume without proportional value.

The audit of kopmanbuild.com's estate found the following. These are measurements from the live source, not estimates.

The 98.2% figure is the one that matters. Normalising the city name and diffing basement-renovation-scarborough against basement-renovation-brampton leaves seven differing line pairs out of 398 — one descriptor sentence, one FAQ sentence, and the areaServed array. Word counts across four sampled cities were 2,326 / 2,327 / 2,328 / 2,329.

A 10 × 60 cross-product is the highest-risk programmatic shape there is, because it produces 60 near-identical pages per service and 10 per neighbourhood. A 60-page location set is defensible. A 600-page cross-product with 14 unique lines per page is not.

Sequence this correctly

Prune Kopman Build before kopman.properties launches and before the group entity links them together. An entity link from a domain carrying this risk is a liability transferred to two new domains, not an asset shared with them.

Remediation, in order

  1. Fix the contact form first. It shows a success message and discards the enquiry. Nothing else on this list matters while the site cannot receive a lead.
  2. Install analytics. Every recommendation below needs a baseline, and there is currently none.
  3. Pull Search Console data and audit by cell. How many of the 630 have had zero impressions in 90 days? Has Kopman actually performed that service in that neighbourhood? A page for work never done there is a doorway page by intent and an experience liability.
  4. Prune hard. Remove or de-index every cell with no project history and no impressions. A 250-page estate where every page is real beats 691 where 400 are filler — and the pruning is itself a positive quality signal.
  5. Inject proprietary data into the survivors. Each surviving location page needs at least one of: a named completed project with photographs, real permit turnaround days for that municipality, local cost data, a real client quote, or a genuine local regulatory note. Toronto, Vaughan and Muskoka differ materially on permits, conservation authority review, septic and heritage — that difference is the content.
  6. Wire the estate into the guides. Every location page should link into the cost index and permit timeline with a locality-specific data cut. That makes the hub genuinely valuable and gives each spoke a reason to exist.
  7. Fix the orphans and the 404s. Build real internal paths to every page that survives; remove the 60 blog URLs from the sitemap or write the posts.
  8. Generate Open Graph images. Per page, showing the actual work.
And for Kopman Properties — do not repeat the pattern

With eight target submarkets and a handful of buildings, the honest page count is 40–80 pages of real substance, not 400 of template: eight market pages carrying live quarterly data, one rich page per owned asset, and the dock-retrofit advisory cluster. The market pages are the only programmatic element, and they survive precisely because they carry statistics no model can reconstruct.

Sheet 04.6  —  Entity

Making "Kopman" one thing

This is where schema still earns its keep. The goal is that a search engine, and a language model reading its output, resolves all three domains to a single organisation.

Two details that are wrong on most sites

Do not mark Kopman Properties as RealEstateAgent. That subtype implies brokerage. If Properties is a principal and owner-operator rather than a licensed brokerage, the markup is a misrepresentation — and in Ontario it is a RECO compliance question as much as a schema one. Put it to counsel before launch, because the same answer governs how every public-facing page describes the business.

Use the Wikidata Linked Data URI (http://www.wikidata.org/entity/Qxxxx), not the browser /wiki/ path — entity resolution expects the former. And a sameAs array pointing at dead URLs declares equivalence to nothing; audit it annually.

Wikidata — achievable, and in this order

Wikidata has no Wikipedia-style notability bar, and it is considerably more attainable than a Wikipedia page while feeding the same Knowledge GraphSource. But external references must exist before the item, or it gets deleted:

  1. Secure 3–5 durable third-party references — Ontario corporate registry, a RENX or Daily Commercial News mention, a BILD or OHBA membership listing, a completed-project write-up.
  2. Create the Kopman Group item citing those.
  3. Create child items for Build and Properties with parent/subsidiary statements.
  4. Add official website statements to each.
  5. Mirror all three into sameAs across the three domains.

Expect 6–12 months before a Knowledge Panel appears, if one appears at all.

Google Business Profile — two, not three

Where two brands operate independently at one address, separate profiles are permittedSource. If Build and Properties are genuinely separate entities with separate phone lines, staff and signage, they are entitled to one profile each at the shared head office. Expect video verification and be ready to demonstrate the independence.

Do not create a third profile for Kopman Group

A holding company with no walk-in customers does not qualify, and it looks like precisely the pattern Google is currently enforcing against. Let the parent live in the Knowledge Graph, not in the local pack.

Do not create a Muskoka address that is not staffed

If Kopman Build has a genuine staffed Muskoka location, that is a legitimate second profile. If it is service-area only, configure it as a service area on the existing profile. A fabricated address is the single highest-risk action available anywhere in this plan.

Sheet 04.7  —  Measurement

Set the contract before the work starts

What to expect

AI referral traffic runs around 1% of sessions on most business sites, and only 14% of marketers track it separately. ChatGPT's share of AI referrals fell from 87.4% to 62.6% between late 2025 and early 2026, with Claude at 18.5%, Gemini 10.6% and Perplexity 7.3%Source — so instrument for several, not one.

Why it still justifies itself

AI referrals convert at roughly 4–5× organicSource. On a $2M–$20M industrial transaction, one qualified inbound pays for the entire programme. Do not let anyone benchmark this on session volume.

The four things to actually track

  1. Citation share against a fixed prompt panel. Write 30 target prompts a real buyer or vendor would ask — "who buys obsolete warehouses in the GTA", "cost to add a loading dock in Ontario", "custom home builder in Rosedale" — and check monthly across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overviews. This, not traffic, is the primary metric.
  2. AI crawler hits in server logs. A leading indicator that runs months ahead of referral traffic. If GPTBot and ClaudeBot are not fetching, nothing downstream will happen.
  3. Branded mention volume. The 0.664 correlation signal. Track mentions of "Kopman" across the web and on YouTube — this is the lever with the highest measured leverage.
  4. A GA4 AI-referral channel group — source matching chatgpt|perplexity|claude|gemini|copilot. Roughly ten minutes of work; without it these sessions misattribute to direct traffic and the programme looks like it did nothing.

The strategy in one paragraph

Build an entity hub, not a link ring — brand anchors up and down, editorial sibling links only where a named project genuinely connects two domains. Prune Kopman Build's 630 pages to the ones that are real before linking anything to them. Do not fight aggregators for transactional industrial keywords; own the dock-retrofit corpus instead, which is verifiably empty in Canada, sits exactly where the two businesses meet, and is backed by cost data only this group possesses. Then spend the marginal dollar on getting "Kopman" mentioned by other people rather than on publishing another page — because that is what the evidence says actually determines whether a machine recommends you.
