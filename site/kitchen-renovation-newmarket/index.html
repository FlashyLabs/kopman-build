Why Newmarket homeowners choose Kopman Build for kitchen renovations

A kitchen renovation is the most technically complex room in the house. It involves plumbing, electrical, gas, ventilation, cabinetry installation, tile work, and countertop fabrication — all of which must coordinate precisely. In Newmarket's detached family homes and newer builds, original kitchens are often small, poorly ventilated, and not laid out for how families actually cook.

Kopman Build manages all trades under one contract. We don't subcontract the project and disappear — the same team that plans your Newmarket kitchen is on site during construction. Layout decisions, material selections, and scope changes are handled directly, without a game of telephone between separate contractors.

Every kitchen project begins with a site consultation to assess the existing conditions — electrical capacity, plumbing rough-in locations, ventilation options, and structural constraints. We price the project accurately before any commitment, including allowances for the surprises common in Newmarket's older homes.

Questions about kitchen renovation newmarket

How much does a kitchen renovation cost in Newmarket?

Kitchen renovation costs in Newmarket typically run $65,000–$120,000 for a mid-range update of an existing layout. A full reconfiguration — opening the kitchen, relocating plumbing, and rebuilding from scratch — runs $120,000–$250,000+. Costs vary based on kitchen size, finish selections, and the condition of existing plumbing and electrical. We provide a detailed line-item estimate before commitment.

Does a kitchen renovation in Newmarket require a permit?

Cosmetic updates — new cabinets in the same location, countertop replacement, paint — don't require a permit. Structural changes (removing walls), plumbing relocation, or electrical panel upgrades do require a City permit. In Newmarket's older homes, it's common to encounter knob-and-tube wiring and cast iron plumbing that need upgrading as part of the renovation — we advise on all of this at the outset.

How long does a kitchen renovation take in Newmarket?

A standalone kitchen renovation in Newmarket typically takes 8–14 weeks once materials are on-site. Cabinetry lead times alone run 6–12 weeks, so the planning and order phase precedes construction. When the kitchen is part of a broader renovation, timelines extend accordingly. We provide a complete schedule at the pre-construction stage.

Do you renovate kitchens in Newmarket?

Yes — kitchen renovation is one of our core services in Newmarket and a growing York Region town. We're familiar with the housing stock, permit requirements, and the material suppliers and trades who work in this area. Contact us to schedule a site consultation.
