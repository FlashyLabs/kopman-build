Questions about renovation contractor markham

What is the cost of a home renovation in Markham?

Typical renovation costs in Markham: kitchens $60,000–$150,000+, bathrooms $25,000–$75,000, basement finishing $55,000–$125,000. We give you a detailed estimate before any commitment.

Do you handle permits for Markham renovations?

Yes. The City of Markham Building Department issues permits for structural work, additions, and conversions. We manage all permits, inspections, and compliance requirements.

Can you renovate heritage properties in Unionville?

Yes. Unionville's heritage district requires sensitivity to original materials and potential approvals from the City of Markham Heritage Committee. We have experience navigating these requirements.

What neighbourhoods in Markham do you serve?

We serve all of Markham including Unionville, Cornell, Cathedraltown, Thornlea, Berczy Village, Greensborough, and Markham Village. No location in Markham is outside our standard service area.
