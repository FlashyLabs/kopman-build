Questions about custom home builder oakville

How much does a custom home cost to build in Oakville?

Custom home construction costs in Oakville typically range from $450 to $900+ per square foot depending on size, design complexity, and finish specification. A 4,000 sq ft custom home in Oakville often runs $1.8M–$3.6M in construction costs alone, not including land. We provide full pre-construction estimates before any commitment.

How long does it take to build a custom home in Oakville?

From consultation to move-in, most Oakville custom homes take 18–30 months. This includes 4–8 months of design and permitting with the Town of Oakville, and 12–22 months of construction depending on size and complexity.

Do you build on lakefront lots in Oakville?

Yes. We have experience with lakefront and waterfront-adjacent properties including setback requirements, Conservation Halton permits where applicable, and the specific structural requirements of sites near the lake.
