General Contractor Danforth Village

Danforth Village's 1920s–1940s semis and bungalows — spanning the corridor from Greektown to Woodbine — are the neighbourhood's defining asset and its greatest renovation opportunity. Why Danforth Village families choose Kopman Build

Danforth Village is a family renovation market. Owners who love the neighbourhood and the Danforth corridor choose to expand in place rather than move — and that means tackling projects that demand structural knowledge, permit experience, and a GC who understands older Toronto housing stock.

We have delivered projects on the Danforth's compact lots for over 25 years. Three things distinguish working here: the 1920s–1940s construction means hidden structural conditions surface regularly — a GC who can read a century-old house is essential. The lots are narrow and semi-detached, so party-wall and drainage logistics require careful coordination with neighbours. And the family market means projects stay occupied during construction — scheduling and dust management matter.

Dustin Kopman founded the company in 1999 and remains personally involved in every project. That hands-on ownership means the person who commits to your project is the same person who delivers it.

Questions about general contracting in Danforth Village

What general contracting work is most common in Danforth Village?

Second-storey additions and open-concept kitchen renovations are the most requested projects on Danforth Village's stock of 1920s–1940s bungalows and semis. Families staying in the neighbourhood typically add a full upper floor rather than move, and the original closed-off kitchens are prime candidates for structural wall removals that open the main floor.

Do older Danforth Village homes require special permits or structural work?

Yes. Homes built in the 1920s and 1940s often require updated structural assessments before a second-storey addition or wall removal. Toronto Building requires permits for additions, structural changes, and underpinning. Kopman Build manages the full permit process, including engineering consultants, so nothing falls through the cracks.

How much does a second-storey addition cost on a Danforth Village bungalow?

A full second-storey addition on a Danforth Village bungalow typically ranges from $350,000 to $600,000 depending on the footprint, finish level, and structural condition of the existing foundation. Kopman Build provides a detailed line-item estimate before any commitment, so you have a clear number going in.

Is Kopman Build licensed and insured to work in Danforth Village?

Yes. Kopman Build is fully licensed and insured for construction work throughout Toronto, including Danforth Village. We carry general liability, builders risk insurance, and maintain WSIB compliance. All documentation is available on request.
