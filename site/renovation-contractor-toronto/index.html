Common questions about home renovations in Toronto

How much does a home renovation cost in Toronto?

Renovation costs in Toronto vary significantly by scope. A kitchen renovation typically runs $60,000–$150,000+. A full bathroom remodel averages $30,000–$80,000. A full-house renovation in the GTA can range from $250,000 to $800,000+ depending on the size of the home and level of finish. We provide detailed, itemised budgets before any work begins.

Do I need a permit for my Toronto renovation?

Most structural renovations, additions, and changes to plumbing or electrical systems require a City of Toronto building permit. Cosmetic work like flooring, painting, and cabinet replacement typically does not. We manage all permit applications and inspections as part of the project.

How long does a home renovation take in Toronto?

A kitchen renovation typically takes 6–12 weeks. A full bathroom remodel is 4–8 weeks. A full-house renovation can take 4–12 months depending on scope. Permit timelines in Toronto add 4–10 weeks to the front end of projects that require them.

Can you renovate while we live in the home?

In many cases, yes. We work with clients to stage work in a way that keeps the home liveable where possible. For major full-house renovations, temporary relocation is sometimes advisable and we'll tell you honestly when that's the case.

Do you serve areas outside Toronto proper?

Yes. We regularly work throughout the GTA including Mississauga, Oakville, Etobicoke, North York, Scarborough, Richmond Hill, and Markham — as well as the Muskoka region for cottage renovations and seasonal property upgrades.
