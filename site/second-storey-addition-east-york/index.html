Second Storey Addition · Toronto — East York

Second Storey Addition East York

East York's dense bungalow market — filled with 1940s–1960s Wartime homes and heavily renovated post-war stock — makes second-storey additions one of the most cost-effective ways to gain space without moving. Kopman Build has been building second-storey additions on Toronto bungalows since 1999, managing everything from structural assessment through final permit.

Why East York clients choose Kopman Build

East York's housing stock is overwhelmingly bungalows and Wartime homes built between the 1940s and 1960s. These homes were constructed to the standards of their era — modest foundation depths, simplified framing, and single-storey roof lines — which means adding a second storey requires genuine structural expertise, not just construction experience. Kopman Build starts every project with a thorough structural and zoning review so there are no surprises once work begins.

The neighbourhood's heavily renovated stock also means each project is unique: previous additions, altered load paths, and non-original materials all affect how a second storey gets engineered and built. Our team is experienced with exactly this kind of layered complexity, and we document it properly so trades and inspectors have what they need.

With land values in East York at a premium and bungalow lots rarely exceeding 25 by 120 feet, going up is frequently a better financial decision than buying a larger home. Kopman Build gives clients a straight-talking feasibility assessment before any design fees are spent.

Second storey addition services in East York

Full Second Storey

Complete second-storey addition over the existing bungalow footprint — typically adding 800–1,200 sq ft of living area with bedrooms, bathrooms, and hallway.

Partial Pop-Up Addition

Second storey added over a portion of the ground floor — a cost-effective option when a full addition exceeds budget or when zoning limits height across the entire footprint.

Structural Assessment & Engineering

Pre-design structural review of existing foundations, walls, and framing — essential for East York's older Wartime housing stock before any design is committed to.

Permit Management

Full City of Toronto permit management: drawings, structural engineer coordination, zoning compliance review, and inspection scheduling.

Interior Integration

Staircase design and construction, reconfigured main-floor layout, and mechanical/electrical upgrades to serve the new upper level.

Basement Legal Suites

Many East York clients combine a second storey addition with a basement legal suite — maximising the property's potential in a single project.

Questions about second storey additions in East York

How much does a second storey addition cost in East York?

A full second storey addition on an East York bungalow typically runs $350,000–$600,000+, depending on the footprint, structural requirements, and finish level. East York's 1940s–1960s Wartime homes often need upgraded foundations and structural reinforcement before a second storey can be added, which should be factored into the budget. Kopman Build provides a detailed pre-construction estimate — with line items and contingencies — before any commitment.

Can my East York bungalow support a second storey?

Most East York bungalows can support a second storey, but structural assessment is essential. Wartime homes from the 1940s and 1950s were built to modest standards; the existing walls, foundations, and roof framing all need to be evaluated by a structural engineer before design begins. Kopman Build coordinates this assessment as part of the pre-construction process.

Do I need a permit for a second storey addition in East York?

Yes. A second storey addition in East York requires a City of Toronto building permit, architectural drawings, and a structural engineer's sign-off. Zoning compliance — including lot coverage, height limits, and setbacks under the applicable zoning bylaw — must be confirmed. In some cases a Committee of Adjustment minor variance is required. Kopman Build manages the full permit and approval process.

How long does a second storey addition take in East York?

From initial consultation to occupancy, expect 12–18 months in total. Permitting in Toronto typically takes 4–8 months; construction of the second storey itself takes 6–10 months once permits are in hand. We give clients a realistic total schedule from day one — not the optimistic version.
