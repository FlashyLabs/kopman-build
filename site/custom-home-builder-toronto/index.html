Common questions about custom home builds in Toronto

How much does a custom home cost to build in Toronto?

Custom home costs in Toronto and the GTA typically range from $400 to $800+ per square foot depending on finishes, site conditions, and design complexity. A 3,000 sq ft custom home often falls in the $1.5M–$2.5M range for construction costs alone, not including land. We provide detailed pre-construction budgets before any commitment is made.

How long does it take to build a custom home in Toronto?

From initial consultation to move-in, most custom homes in the GTA take 18–30 months. This includes 4–8 months of design and permitting, and 12–20 months of construction depending on size and complexity. Toronto building permits typically take 3–6 months to obtain.

Do you build custom homes in Muskoka?

Yes. We have extensive experience building custom homes and cottages throughout the Muskoka region — including on waterfront lots, which carry specific shoreline and setback requirements we are experienced navigating.

Do I need my own architect, or can Kopman Build coordinate one?

Both. We work comfortably alongside clients who have an existing architect relationship, and we can also recommend architects we have worked with repeatedly and trust. Coordination between design and construction is something we manage actively.

What makes Kopman Build different from other Toronto home builders?

Most builders are either large volume operators or small crews without systems. Kopman Build is neither — we bring the discipline of a firm with over 25 years and 400+ projects to a personal, owner-direct engagement. Dustin Kopman is directly involved in every project.
