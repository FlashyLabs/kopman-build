Questions about basement renovation toronto

How much does a basement renovation cost in Toronto?

Basement finishing in Toronto typically costs $60,000–$130,000 for a standard finish. A legal secondary suite runs $80,000–$150,000+, including plumbing, fire separation, egress windows, separate mechanical, and permit fees. Underpinning adds $30,000–$80,000+ depending on depth and scope. We provide detailed estimates before any commitment.

Do you need a permit to finish a basement in Toronto?

Yes. Any basement renovation involving structural changes, new walls, electrical, plumbing, or secondary suite creation requires a building permit from the City of Toronto. Kopman Build manages all permit applications, required inspections, and final sign-offs.

What is required for a legal basement suite in Toronto?

Toronto's Second Suite bylaw requires: a minimum ceiling height (typically 1.95m), egress window in sleeping areas, fire separation between units (fire-rated assemblies), separate HVAC or dedicated heating, an accessible entrance, and smoke and CO detector requirements. We build to these standards and permit and inspect all work.

How long does a basement renovation take in Toronto?

A standard basement finish takes 8–14 weeks. A legal secondary suite typically takes 12–18 weeks due to the additional inspections and systems work required. Underpinning adds 4–8 weeks to the schedule before finishing can begin.
