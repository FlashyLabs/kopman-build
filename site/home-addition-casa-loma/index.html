Home Addition · Casa Loma, Toronto

Home Addition Casa Loma

Casa Loma's early 20th century homes on hillside ravine lots along the Spadina corridor are some of Toronto's most architecturally distinctive — and extending them takes a contractor who understands the structural and topographical demands that come with the territory. Kopman Build has been building home additions on complex Toronto sites since 1999, from initial feasibility through final permit sign-off.

Why Casa Loma clients choose Kopman Build

Casa Loma's dramatic lot topography changes how every home addition is designed and built. Grade differentials along the hillside mean foundation approaches that work on a flat Annex lot simply do not apply here — structural engineers, retaining wall specialists, and sometimes geotechnical consultants must be engaged from the planning stage. Kopman Build coordinates this entire consultant team so nothing is missed.

The neighbourhood's ravine proximity also brings the Toronto and Region Conservation Authority (TRCA) into many projects. Properties within or near the Ravine and Natural Feature Protection overlay require TRCA review before the City will issue permits — a step that many contractors overlook until it causes costly delays. We build that review into our project timeline from day one.

Casa Loma's housing stock dates predominantly from the early 1900s. That means knob-and-tube wiring, older plumbing systems, and in some cases, materials requiring abatement. Kopman Build assesses the existing conditions of your home at the outset and builds remediation into the project scope and budget so there are no surprises once walls open up.

Questions about home additions in Casa Loma

How does the hillside topography in Casa Loma affect a home addition?

Casa Loma's ravine-adjacent hillside lots present real engineering challenges — grade changes, retaining walls, and soil conditions all affect foundation design and structural approach for any addition. Kopman Build conducts a thorough site assessment before scoping any project, and we coordinate geotechnical and structural engineering from the outset to avoid surprises mid-build.

Do I need a permit for a home addition in Casa Loma?

Yes. Home additions in Casa Loma require a building permit from the City of Toronto. Given the neighbourhood's ravine proximity and many properties within the Ravine and Natural Feature Protection overlay, TRCA review may also be required before permits are issued. Kopman Build manages the full permit and approval process, including any required conservation authority consultations.

How much does a home addition cost on an early 20th century Casa Loma home?

Older homes along the Spadina corridor often require additional structural remediation — upgraded foundations, knob-and-tube removal, asbestos management — before new addition work begins. Budget $220,000–$450,000+ for a rear addition and $350,000–$650,000+ for a full second-storey addition on a heritage-era Casa Loma property. Kopman Build provides a detailed pre-construction estimate before any commitment.

Can I add a second storey to my Casa Loma bungalow or semi-detached home?

In most cases, yes — second-storey additions are a popular choice in Casa Loma given the neighbourhood's large, well-established lots and active full-renovation market. Feasibility depends on existing structural conditions, zoning setbacks, and any heritage designation on your property. Kopman Build will assess your home and provide a clear picture of what's achievable before you invest further.
