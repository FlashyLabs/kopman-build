Why Parkdale clients choose Kopman Build

Parkdale is one of Toronto's most architecturally distinctive neighbourhoods — a dense concentration of Victorian and Edwardian row houses, many with active heritage designations and original details still intact. Renovating here means working within the City's heritage permit framework, sourcing period-appropriate materials, and maintaining the character of buildings that are over a century old. It also means understanding the structural realities of houses built long before modern building code.

As Parkdale continues to evolve, demand has grown for renovations that honour the Victorian bones of these homes while delivering fully modern kitchens, bathrooms, and open-plan living spaces. Kopman Build has the experience to do both at once — restoring original plaster mouldings and millwork while reconfiguring layouts and upgrading every system behind the walls.

Dustin Kopman founded the company in 1999 after growing up working in construction through his family's firm, G& G Enterprise. That hands-on foundation means every Parkdale project is led by someone who genuinely understands the craft — and the heritage responsibilities that come with it.

Questions about renovation contractor Parkdale

How much does a home renovation cost in Parkdale?

Kitchen renovations in Parkdale typically run $65,000–$160,000+ depending on layout changes and finish selections. Bathroom renovations range $28,000–$80,000. Victorian whole-home renovations — which often include structural and heritage considerations — generally start at $200,000 and can exceed $600,000 for full gut-and-restore projects. We provide detailed pre-construction estimates before any commitment.

Do you need permits for renovations in Parkdale?

Yes. Parkdale falls under City of Toronto jurisdiction, so structural renovations, additions, and basement conversions require building permits. Properties in Parkdale's heritage designation areas may also need Heritage Permit approval from the City before work begins. Kopman Build manages the full permit process — applications, heritage consultations, inspections, and final sign-offs.

Can you work on heritage-designated properties in Parkdale?

Yes. Parkdale has a significant number of properties under heritage designation or within heritage conservation districts. Kopman Build has experience navigating Toronto's heritage permitting process, sourcing period-appropriate materials, and restoring original Victorian details — plaster mouldings, millwork, and masonry — while integrating modern kitchens, bathrooms, and mechanical systems behind the scenes.

How long does a Victorian row house renovation take in Parkdale?

A kitchen renovation typically takes 8–14 weeks. A bathroom 4–8 weeks. A full Victorian row house renovation — including structural work, systems upgrades, and heritage restoration — generally runs 6–18 months depending on scope and permit timelines. Heritage permit approvals can add 4–8 weeks to the pre-construction phase. We build realistic schedules that account for this from the outset.

Do you handle the conversion of Parkdale rooming houses or multi-unit buildings?

Yes. Many Parkdale properties were historically divided into rooming houses or multi-unit configurations. We work with owners to consolidate back to single-family use, reconfigure layouts, upgrade electrical and plumbing to current code, and restore the original character of the building. These projects require careful permitting and structural assessment, and we manage the full process end to end.
