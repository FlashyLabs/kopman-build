Design-Build Lawrence Park

Lawrence Park's 1920s and 1930s Arts and Crafts and Tudor Revival homes on generous ravine lots demand a level of design sensitivity that only works when the people drawing the plans are directly accountable for building them. Kopman Build's integrated design-build model brings architecture, permits, and construction under one team — so your renovation respects the character of the house and stays on budget from the first meeting to final handover.

Why Lawrence Park clients choose Kopman Build

Lawrence Park is one of Toronto's most carefully preserved early-twentieth-century neighbourhoods. The homes here — Arts and Crafts bungalows, Tudor Revival two-storeys, brick and stucco semis on wide lots — carry period details that reward careful restoration and punish careless renovation. The design-build model is the right fit because design decisions and construction knowledge are never separated: when our team specifies a material or a structural approach, they know exactly how it will be built and what it will cost.

Heritage considerations add a layer of complexity that a fragmented architect-plus-contractor arrangement handles poorly. Some properties carry formal heritage designation; many more sit within the neighbourhood's established character overlay. Kopman Build's team navigates those constraints as part of the design process, not as a late-stage obstacle. The result is fewer permit rejections, fewer expensive field changes, and a renovation that the City approves and the neighbourhood respects.

Dustin Kopman founded the company in 1999 after growing up in construction through his family's firm, G&G Enterprise. Every Lawrence Park project is managed by someone with the craft knowledge to make good decisions on site — not just someone who can produce drawings.

Questions about design-build in Lawrence Park

How does the design-build process account for Lawrence Park's heritage character?

Lawrence Park contains properties with heritage designation as well as many homes that fall under the neighbourhood's established character guidelines. Our design team works within those constraints from the outset — not as an afterthought — so that design proposals are realistic before any drawings are submitted to the City. This early alignment avoids costly revisions later in the permit process.

Can design-build work on a 1920s or 1930s Arts and Crafts or Tudor Revival home?

Yes — and Lawrence Park's historic housing stock is exactly where an integrated model adds the most value. Older homes frequently reveal structural conditions, knob-and-tube wiring, or plaster details that require on-the-fly design decisions. Because design and construction sit under one team, those decisions are made quickly and documented properly, rather than creating a standoff between a separate architect and contractor.

We want to finish the basement for an in-law suite. Is that a design-build scope?

Absolutely. Basement suites in Lawrence Park involve layout planning, egress window design, mechanical separation, and permit applications — all of which benefit from a single coordinated team. We handle the design, City approvals, and full construction delivery, so there is no coordination gap between who drew the plans and who builds them.

What custom kitchen and bathroom work do you handle in Lawrence Park?

We design and build custom kitchens and bathrooms as fully integrated scopes — cabinetry specification, material selection, plumbing and electrical coordination, and all trades managed by our site team. In Lawrence Park homes, where period details like built-in cabinetry or original tilework sometimes need to be respected or matched, having design and construction under one roof makes the difference between a renovation that fits the house and one that fights it.
