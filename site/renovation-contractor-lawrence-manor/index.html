Renovation Contractor Lawrence Manor

Lawrence Manor's 1950s bungalows are some of the most sought-after renovation opportunities in North York — solid bones, generous lots, and strong resale demand near Lawrence station. Kopman Build specialises in the bungalow additions and whole-home renovations that define this neighbourhood, bringing 25 years of construction discipline to every project on Lawrence and Bathurst.

Why Lawrence Manor clients choose Kopman Build

Lawrence Manor is a compact, established North York neighbourhood where nearly every project starts with a 1950s bungalow. Renovating these homes well requires understanding post-war construction — aging electrical, original plumbing, and modest ceiling heights — before a single wall opens. Kopman Build has worked on dozens of homes just like yours in this pocket of North York.

  • Deep familiarity with 1950s bungalow construction: we know what to expect when walls open — knob-and-tube wiring, galvanized plumbing, and shallow foundations — and we price and plan accordingly so there are no surprises mid-project.
  • Addition expertise on compact lots: Lawrence Manor properties sit on tighter footprints that demand creative design. We maximise livable area through rear extensions and second-storey additions engineered to meet Toronto zoning and Ontario Building Code.
  • Proximity to Lawrence station keeps the neighbourhood active and values strong — renovations here deliver consistent ROI, and our team coordinates deliveries and trades efficiently on residential streets without disrupting neighbours.

Questions about renovating in Lawrence Manor

How much does a bungalow renovation cost in Lawrence Manor?

A full bungalow renovation in Lawrence Manor — updated kitchen, bathrooms, basement, and mechanical systems — typically runs $200,000–$450,000 depending on scope and finishes. Kitchen-only renovations range $60,000–$140,000. Bathroom renovations run $25,000–$70,000. We provide a detailed line-item pre-construction estimate before any commitment.

Can I add a second storey to my Lawrence Manor bungalow?

Yes. Second-storey additions are one of the most popular projects we complete in Lawrence Manor. Most 1950s bungalows in the neighbourhood can structurally support an addition once the foundation and main floor framing are assessed. Toronto zoning generally permits additions up to the allowable height and lot coverage for the R-zone. Kopman Build manages the full design, permit, and construction process.

Do I need a permit to renovate my Lawrence Manor home?

Most structural renovations, additions, and basement conversions in Lawrence Manor fall under City of Toronto jurisdiction and require a building permit. This includes second-storey additions, rear additions, underpinning, and any work touching electrical or plumbing. Kopman Build handles all permit applications, inspections, and sign-offs on your behalf.

How does a rear addition work on a Lawrence Manor lot?

Lawrence Manor lots are typically compact but well-suited to rear additions extending the main-floor footprint. Toronto zoning sets limits on lot coverage and rear-yard setbacks, so the buildable addition area varies by property. Kopman Build reviews your specific lot dimensions and zoning conditions during the consultation, then coordinates design drawings and permit approval before any ground is broken.

How long does a whole-home bungalow renovation take in Lawrence Manor?

A full bungalow renovation including kitchen, bathrooms, basement, and mechanical updates typically runs 5–8 months from permit approval to handover. A second-storey addition adds 3–4 months on top of the base scope. We build realistic schedules at the outset and track progress weekly so there are no surprises.
