Why Bloor West Village clients choose Kopman Build

Bloor West Village is one of Toronto's most established family neighbourhoods — a place where homeowners invest in their properties for the long term. The area's character housing stock, largely built between the 1930s and 1950s, responds well to thoughtful laneway house additions: deep rear yards, mature laneways, and solid brick construction that holds its value for generations.

Building a laneway house here means navigating the City's Laneway Suite Guidelines, coordinating with Toronto Building, and managing the technical realities of working on older properties — rear yard drainage, brick foundation compatibility, and utility connections from the lane. Kopman Build brings 25 years of Toronto construction experience to every one of these challenges.

Dustin Kopman founded the company in 1999 after growing up in construction through his family's firm, G& G Enterprise. That hands-on background means your project is managed by someone who understands the craft — not just the contract.

Questions about laneway houses in Bloor West Village

Is my Bloor West Village property eligible for a laneway house?

Many Bloor West Village lots are eligible — the neighbourhood's 1930s and 1950s-era detached and semi-detached homes typically back onto rear laneways that meet the City's minimum 3.5 m width requirement. Eligibility also depends on your lot depth, rear setbacks, and any existing structures. Kopman Build will assess your specific property during a free consultation.

How much does a laneway house cost in Bloor West Village?

A laneway house in Bloor West Village typically costs between $280,000 and $480,000 depending on size, finishes, and site conditions. Older brick homes in the neighbourhood may require additional structural work at the rear yard. Kopman Build provides a detailed pre-construction estimate before any commitment.

What permits are required to build a laneway house in Toronto?

A laneway house in Toronto requires a building permit, a site plan drawing, and compliance with the City's Laneway Suite Guidelines. In most cases no Committee of Adjustment approval is needed if the design meets as-of-right zoning requirements. Kopman Build manages all permit applications, engineering consultants, and City correspondence from start to finish.

How long does it take to build a laneway house in Bloor West Village?

From initial consultation to occupancy, expect 14 to 20 months total. Permitting alone typically takes 4 to 8 months; construction runs approximately 8 to 12 months after permit approval. Kopman Build gives you a realistic project timeline from day one so there are no surprises.
