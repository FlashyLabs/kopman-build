Kitchen Renovation Mimico

Mimico's 1930s–1960s bungalows and semis are among the most compelling renovation candidates in south Etobicoke — lakefront proximity and rapid gentrification mean a well-executed kitchen renovation delivers both a transformed home and lasting market value. Kopman Build has been renovating kitchens across Etobicoke and the GTA for over 25 years, with deep experience opening up and modernizing the compact floor plans that define this neighbourhood's housing stock.

Why Mimico clients choose Kopman Build

Mimico's post-war bungalows are full of potential — and full of complexity. The compartmentalized kitchens typical of homes built between the 1930s and 1960s require careful structural assessment before any wall comes down. Load-bearing configurations, aging electrical panels, and galvanized plumbing all factor into a kitchen renovation in this neighbourhood. Kopman Build manages every trade and every milestone in-house, so nothing falls through the cracks between your contractor and your subcontractors.

Gentrification has raised the bar in Mimico. Buyers arriving from downtown Toronto and the waterfront bring expectations that match what they left — open layouts, high-spec finishes, and appliance packages that belong in a custom home. We build kitchens that meet that standard, in homes that were never designed for it.

Dustin Kopman founded the company in 1999 after growing up working in construction through his family's firm, G& G Enterprise. Every Mimico project is managed by someone who understands the craft — not just the sales pitch.

Questions about kitchen renovation Mimico

How much does a kitchen renovation cost in Mimico?

Kitchen renovation costs in Mimico reflect both the neighbourhood's typical housing scale and its rapid gentrification. A cosmetic refresh in a Mimico bungalow (new countertops, hardware, cabinet refinishing) runs $18,000–$38,000. A mid-range full kitchen renovation with new cabinetry and layout adjustments runs $65,000–$115,000. A full custom kitchen with structural wall removal, premium finishes, and appliance integration runs $130,000–$260,000+. We provide a detailed line-item estimate before any commitment.

Can Kopman Build open up the kitchen in my 1940s Mimico bungalow?

Yes — this is one of the most common projects we undertake in Mimico and across south Etobicoke. The post-war bungalows in this area typically have compact, compartmentalized kitchens that respond very well to open-concept conversions. We assess load-bearing walls, arrange any required engineering, and obtain City of Toronto permits before touching a single wall. The result is a dramatically more liveable and marketable home.

Do kitchen renovations in Mimico require permits?

Any structural work — removing or modifying load-bearing walls, which is very common in Mimico's bungalow stock — requires a City of Toronto building permit. Plumbing relocations and electrical panel upgrades also require permits. A cosmetic kitchen update with no structural, plumbing, or electrical changes typically does not. Kopman Build manages all permit applications and engineering consultants on your behalf.

Is a kitchen renovation a good investment in Mimico right now?

Mimico is one of the stronger renovation value markets in the GTA. Lakefront proximity, transit access, and rapid gentrification are driving sustained demand and price appreciation. A well-executed kitchen renovation in a Mimico bungalow or semi typically yields strong ROI at resale — particularly open-concept conversions that modernize the floor plan. We can discuss realistic return expectations at your consultation.
