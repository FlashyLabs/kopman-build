How much does a home renovation cost in Toronto in 2026?

By Dustin Kopman · Kopman Build · Toronto, GTA & Muskoka

Toronto homeowners ask us this question constantly — and the honest answer is: it depends on more variables than most contractors will admit upfront. What we can give you is a clear, practical breakdown based on what we've seen across 400+ projects in the GTA.

These are 2026 figures based on current material costs, Toronto labour rates, and permit timelines. Use them as a baseline, not a quote. Every project needs a proper scope to price accurately.

Kitchen Renovation Costs in Toronto

Kitchen renovations are the most requested project we receive. Costs vary enormously based on whether you're keeping the layout or changing it, the quality of cabinetry, and whether plumbing or electrical moves are involved.

ScopeTypical Cost Range
Cosmetic refresh (new cabinet doors, countertops, fixtures)$25,000 – $50,000
Mid-range full renovation (same layout)$60,000 – $100,000
Full renovation with layout changes$100,000 – $150,000
High-end custom kitchen$150,000 – $250,000+

What drives kitchen costs up: Moving the plumbing stack, custom cabinetry (vs. semi-custom), appliance grade, stone or quartz countertops, and heated flooring all add meaningfully to the budget.

Bathroom Renovation Costs in Toronto

Bathrooms are priced per room and by scope. A complete gut-and-rebuild of a primary ensuite in Toronto typically lands significantly higher than people expect because of tile labour, plumbing rough-in, and the small-space complexity of fitting everything properly.

ScopeTypical Cost Range
Powder room refresh$10,000 – $20,000
Main bathroom full renovation$30,000 – $60,000
Primary ensuite full renovation$50,000 – $100,000
Primary ensuite with custom tile and steam shower$100,000 – $150,000+

Full-House Renovation Costs in Toronto

A full-house renovation in the GTA — meaning everything touched: kitchen, all bathrooms, flooring, millwork, electrical, plumbing, HVAC — is typically priced by square footage of the home.

Home SizeTypical Total Range
1,500 sq ft semi-detached$300,000 – $600,000
2,500 sq ft detached$500,000 – $900,000
3,500 sq ft+ detached, high finish$900,000 – $1,500,000+

Note on these numbers: Full-house renovations vary dramatically based on existing conditions — knob-and-tube wiring, galvanized plumbing, foundation issues, and asbestos remediation can all add $50,000–$150,000 to a project that looks straightforward on the surface. A proper pre-construction assessment uncovers these before you're committed.

Addition Costs in Toronto

Additions — rear additions, second-storey additions, and garage conversions — are priced per square foot of new space added, plus the integration costs with the existing structure.

TypeTypical Cost Range
Rear main-floor addition (per sq ft)$400 – $700 / sq ft
Second-storey addition (per sq ft)$350 – $600 / sq ft
Deck (pressure-treated, mid-range)$25,000 – $60,000
Deck (composite, custom, large)$60,000 – $120,000+

What affects renovation costs most in Toronto

  • Permit timelines — Toronto building permits typically take 4–10 weeks and add holding costs to any project
  • Existing conditions — older homes (pre-1980) often have outdated electrical, plumbing, or insulation that must be upgraded
  • Labour availability — skilled trades in Toronto are at a premium in 2026; good contractors are booked 3–6 months out
  • Material costs — lumber, steel, and tile costs remain elevated vs. pre-2020 levels
  • Contingency — any honest budget includes a 10–15% contingency; surprises in renovation are the rule, not the exception
