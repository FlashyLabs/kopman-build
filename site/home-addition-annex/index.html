Home Addition The Annex

The Annex is defined by its late Victorian and Edwardian homes built between the 1880s and 1910s — a dense, mature housing stock that rewards careful addition work. Kopman Build has been designing and building home additions in Toronto's heritage character areas since 1999, adding space without erasing what makes these properties worth keeping.

Why Annex clients choose Kopman Build

The Annex presents renovation considerations you will not find in newer Toronto neighbourhoods. The housing stock dates from the 1880s to the 1910s — meaning original brick, plaster walls, knob-and-tube wiring in older sections, and foundations that require careful structural assessment before any addition can be engineered. Kopman Build has the experience to understand what is behind the walls before construction begins.

The neighbourhood's proximity to the University of Toronto and its heritage character area status create a specific permitting environment. Many Annex properties sit within or adjacent to Heritage Conservation Districts, and design review is more rigorous than in standard Toronto neighbourhoods. We manage the full heritage permit and variance process in-house.

The Annex also has an active market for semi-detached conversions back to single-family use, along with strong demand for rear additions that open up kitchen and living spaces to rear gardens. Kopman Build has completed multiple projects in the neighbourhood and understands how the City evaluates addition proposals here.

Questions about home additions in The Annex

Can you add onto a late Victorian or Edwardian home in The Annex without compromising its character?

Yes — and in The Annex it requires genuine care. Kopman Build designs additions to complement the original brick massing, cornice lines, and fenestration patterns typical of the neighbourhood's 1880s–1910s housing stock. We work with architects experienced in heritage-adjacent design so additions read as thoughtful rather than grafted on.

Does The Annex have heritage restrictions that affect home additions?

The Annex is a heritage character area under Toronto's Official Plan, and many streets are within or adjacent to Heritage Conservation Districts. This does not automatically prohibit additions, but it does mean design review is more rigorous. Kopman Build assesses heritage designation status for every Annex property before scoping a project and manages any required Heritage Permit applications.

How much does a home addition cost in The Annex?

A single-storey rear addition in The Annex typically runs $220,000–$420,000+ depending on size and finish level. A second-storey addition on an Annex semi or detached home runs $320,000–$620,000+. Heritage-sensitive detailing and the neighbourhood's narrower lots can affect cost — we provide a detailed pre-construction estimate before any commitment.

Is a home addition a better option than buying a larger property in The Annex?

For most Annex homeowners, yes. Detached and semi-detached properties in The Annex command significant premiums, and land transfer tax plus transaction costs make moving expensive. A well-executed addition can add the space you need at a fraction of the cost of upsizing — and lets you stay in a neighbourhood that is difficult to buy back into.
