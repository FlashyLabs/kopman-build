Questions about general contractor toronto

What is a general contractor responsible for in Toronto?

A general contractor (GC) is responsible for the overall execution of a construction project: managing subcontractors, coordinating permits and inspections, controlling costs and schedule, and serving as the single accountable party. Kopman Build acts as GC on residential and commercial projects throughout Toronto and the GTA.

How do I choose a general contractor in Toronto?

Look for a GC with verifiable experience in your project type, a real project management system (not just a spreadsheet), and a principal who is personally involved. Ask for references and ask those references if the project came in on budget and on schedule. Kopman Build will provide references on request.

Does Kopman Build work on commercial projects?

Yes. We have delivered commercial fit-outs, institutional projects, and mixed-use renovations across Toronto. Our client base includes family offices, institutional developers, interior design firms, and real estate agencies, in addition to private residential clients.

Is Kopman Build licensed and insured in Toronto?

Yes. Kopman Build operates with full licensing and insurance required for construction in Ontario, including general liability, builders risk, and WSIB compliance. All documentation available on request.
