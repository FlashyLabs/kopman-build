Why Princess-Rosethorn clients choose Kopman Build

Princess-Rosethorn is a premium Etobicoke enclave where the housing stock — custom-built bungalows from the 1950s and 1960s on large, well-treed lots — creates ideal conditions for major second storey additions. The lots are generous enough to accommodate the structural work, and the neighbourhood's land values make investing in a full addition financially sound rather than buying into a more expensive property elsewhere.

Second storey additions on mid-century bungalows require careful structural integration. Existing foundations, load-bearing walls, and roof lines all inform the design before a single drawing is produced. Dustin Kopman founded this company in 1999 after years working in his family's construction firm, and that hands-on depth means Princess-Rosethorn clients get a builder who understands the craft — not just the contract.

Kopman Build brings three things that matter specifically in Princess-Rosethorn: deep familiarity with Etobicoke zoning and permit requirements, experience matching addition quality to the neighbourhood's existing architectural standard, and a track record of managing complex structural work on mid-century homes without surprises.

Questions about second storey additions in Princess-Rosethorn

How much does a second storey addition cost in Princess-Rosethorn?

A full second storey addition on a Princess-Rosethorn bungalow typically runs $350,000–$650,000+, depending on the footprint, structural scope, and finish level. The neighbourhood's premium Etobicoke location and the quality expectations of surrounding homes often support upper-range finishes. Kopman Build provides a detailed pre-construction estimate before any commitment is made.

Do the 1950s–1960s bungalows in Princess-Rosethorn support a second storey addition?

Most do, but the existing structure must be assessed before design begins. The custom-built bungalows on generous Princess-Rosethorn lots were built to higher standards than production housing of the same era, which is an advantage — but foundations, wall framing, and load paths still require engineering review. Kopman Build conducts a thorough structural assessment as part of every pre-construction process.

What permits are required for a second storey addition in Etobicoke?

A second storey addition in Princess-Rosethorn requires a building permit from the City of Toronto, full architectural and structural drawings, and a site plan. Depending on how the finished structure relates to Etobicoke zoning bylaws — particularly height and lot coverage limits — a minor variance through the Committee of Adjustment may also be required. Kopman Build manages the complete permit and approval process from application through occupancy.

How long does a second storey addition take in Princess-Rosethorn?

From initial consultation to occupancy, expect 14–20 months in total. Permitting typically takes 4–8 months; construction itself runs 8–14 months depending on scope. Kopman Build provides a realistic project schedule at the outset and tracks against it throughout construction with weekly client updates.
