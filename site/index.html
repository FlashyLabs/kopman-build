§ 04 — Process Seven stages

Every Kopman Build engagement moves through the same disciplined sequence. The deliverables at each stage are made explicit upfront — so progress is measured against the plan, not against impressions.

  1. 01 Consultation Listening session and site walk. We define the project in writing — goals, constraints, timeline, budget envelope. Week 1
  2. 02 Planning Scope of work, design brief, consultant team, permits and approvals strategy. Risks identified before they cost money. Weeks 2–6
  3. 03 Budgeting Itemised pre-construction estimate with allowances, contingencies, and a clear path to cost certainty. Weeks 4–8
  4. 04 Design Coordination Architect, engineers, interiors and specialist trades aligned. Drawings are buildable before they leave the table. Ongoing
  5. 05 Construction Full-time site supervision, weekly client reporting, and live cost-to-complete visibility throughout. Project term
  6. 06 Delivery Punch list, commissioning, owner training, and a structured handover package — drawings, warranties, manuals. Final 4 weeks
  7. 07 Aftercare 12-month attendance window. The relationship doesn’t end at handover — it changes shape. Year 1+
