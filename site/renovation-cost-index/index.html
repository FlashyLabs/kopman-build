Questions about using this index

How accurate are the cost ranges in this index?

The ranges reflect all-in contractor pricing derived from actual project scopes, permit applications filed in Toronto, and supplier material pricing. They represent realistic market ranges, not theoretical estimates. Individual projects may fall outside these ranges depending on site conditions, structural complexity, and client-specific requirements.

Do these costs include design fees and permits?

Yes. All figures in this index are all-in pricing inclusive of design, permits, labour, and materials. They exclude furniture, appliances, and owner-supplied fixtures.

How do neighbourhood premiums work?

Premiums reflect higher labour costs, more complex permit environments, heritage overlay requirements, and elevated client expectations in certain neighbourhoods. A Rosedale kitchen renovation will typically cost 18–28% more than an equivalent project in a standard Toronto neighbourhood due to these compounding factors.

How often is the index updated?

The Kopman Build Cost Index is updated quarterly. The Q3 2026 edition was published in July 2026. The next update is scheduled for October 2026, reflecting Q3 project completions and any material cost movements.

Can I use these figures to plan my renovation budget?

These figures are a reliable starting point for budget planning. For a project-specific estimate, we recommend requesting a formal consultation. Kopman Build provides detailed itemised budgets before any commitments are made — no obligation, no pressure.
