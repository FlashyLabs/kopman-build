Questions about bathroom renovation toronto

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Toronto?

Bathroom renovation costs in Toronto: powder rooms $18,000–$40,000; main bathrooms $30,000–$70,000; primary ensuites $50,000–$150,000+. High-end primary suites with custom tile, freestanding tubs, and imported fixtures can exceed $200,000. We give you a detailed estimate before any decision is made.

How long does a bathroom renovation take in Toronto?

A standard bathroom renovation takes 4–8 weeks once the scope is defined and materials are ordered. A large primary ensuite with custom tile work can take 8–12 weeks. Custom tile installations and steam shower builds add time. We build a realistic schedule and stick to it.

What is proper waterproofing for a shower in Toronto?

Proper shower waterproofing uses a membrane system (Schluter KERDI, RedGard, or similar) applied to the substrate before tile installation. This prevents moisture from reaching the wall assembly behind the tile. Without it, even a properly grouted tile installation can allow water infiltration over time. Every shower we tile is properly waterproofed.

Do bathroom renovations require permits in Toronto?

Plumbing relocation and any structural changes to bathroom walls require City of Toronto permits. A like-for-like bathroom renovation (same fixtures in same locations) may not require a permit, but we assess this on a project-by-project basis and advise accordingly.
