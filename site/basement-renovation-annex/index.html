Basement Renovation · Toronto & The Annex

Basement Renovation The Annex

The Annex's late Victorian and Edwardian homes — built between the 1880s and 1910s, just north of the University of Toronto campus — bring character and proximity, but also low ceilings, rubble-stone foundations, and the structural realities of 100-year-old construction. Kopman Build has been finishing and converting Annex basements for over 25 years, delivering permitted legal secondary suites and finished living spaces that work within the constraints of these period homes and within the requirements of the Heritage Conservation District.

Why Annex clients choose Kopman Build

Basement renovations in The Annex are not standard work. The Victorian and Edwardian housing stock that lines streets like Lowther, Admiral, and Walmer Road was built before modern codes, which means every project involves navigating original rubble stone foundations, knob-and-tube wiring, and ceiling heights that were never designed for habitable space. Getting it right requires a contractor who understands period construction — not just finishing drywall.

The Annex is also a Heritage Conservation District. While interior basement work rarely triggers heritage review on its own, scope that touches the exterior — a separate entrance, egress windows at grade, or a below-grade stairwell — can require Heritage Permit approval alongside a standard building permit. Kopman Build manages both processes and knows from experience which elements of a project need which approvals.

The neighbourhood's proximity to the University of Toronto campus makes legal basement suites an especially strong investment here. Rental demand is consistent, and a permitted, code-compliant suite protects that income long-term. Dustin Kopman founded the company in 1999 after working in his family's construction firm — that background means every project is run by someone who understands the work, not just the sales process.

Questions about basement renovation in The Annex

How much does a basement renovation cost in The Annex?

Basement renovations in The Annex typically cost $65,000–$140,000 for a standard finish in a Victorian or Edwardian semi-detached or detached home. Legal secondary suites run $85,000–$160,000+ due to required fire separation, egress windows, separate mechanical, and permit fees. Many Annex basements also require underpinning to achieve livable ceiling heights, which adds $35,000–$85,000+ to the project. We provide detailed written estimates before any commitment.

Are there heritage restrictions that affect basement renovations in The Annex?

The Annex is designated a Heritage Conservation District under the Ontario Heritage Act. While heritage rules primarily govern changes to exterior facades, basement work that requires modifying the front facade — such as adding a separate entrance with exterior stairs, or altering a front window for egress — may require Heritage Permit approval in addition to a standard building permit. Kopman Build manages both permit streams and understands which scope triggers heritage review.

What are the challenges of finishing a basement in a Victorian or Edwardian home in The Annex?

Homes built in The Annex between the 1880s and 1910s were constructed before modern building codes. Common challenges include low ceiling heights (often under 6 feet), knob-and-tube wiring that must be removed before insulating, original rubble stone or brick foundations requiring waterproofing, and undersized drain lines needing upgrade for a new bathroom. Underpinning or bench-pinning is frequently required to achieve the 1.95m minimum ceiling height mandated for a legal suite. Kopman Build has extensive experience navigating all of these conditions in period homes.

Can a basement suite in The Annex be used as a student rental given the proximity to U of T?

Yes. The Annex's proximity to the University of Toronto campus makes legal basement suites an especially strong rental investment. To legally rent a basement unit, it must comply with Toronto's Second Suite bylaw: minimum ceiling height of 1.95m, egress window in sleeping areas, fire-rated separation between floors, dedicated or separate heating, and a permitted entrance. Kopman Build builds legal suites to these requirements so your rental income is protected by proper permits and inspections.
