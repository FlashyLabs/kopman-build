Second-storey additions in Highland Creek

Highland Creek's post-war and newer detached homes are the primary candidates for second-storey addition. When a family has outgrown a single-storey home but loves their neighbourhood, building up is almost always more cost-effective than buying up.

A second-storey addition is the most structurally complex residential project type. It involves assessing the existing foundation and floor structure for load capacity, removing the existing roof, constructing the new floor and roof system, and integrating new mechanical systems — all while the family often continues living in the lower floor.

Kopman Build manages the full structural assessment, engineering coordination, architectural design, permit process, and construction. In Highland Creek, this includes navigating the City's zoning requirements for height, setbacks, and lot coverage — and identifying any heritage-adjacent complications early in the process.

Questions about second storey addition highland creek

How much does a second-storey addition cost in Highland Creek?

A full second-storey addition in Highland Creek typically costs $350,000–$700,000+ depending on the footprint, finish level, and structural complexity. Partial additions (pop-tops) run $200,000–$400,000. These estimates include all hard construction costs. Soft costs (permits, engineering, architecture) add another $40,000–$80,000 typically. We provide a detailed pre-construction estimate before commitment.

How long does a second-storey addition take in Highland Creek?

A full second-storey addition in Highland Creek typically takes 6–10 months from permit approval to completion. Permit acquisition adds 6–12 weeks. The most disruptive phase — roof removal, structural work, and framing — takes 4–6 weeks. We typically allow families to remain in the lower floor during construction in most cases.

Does a second-storey addition require permits in Highland Creek?

Yes — a second-storey addition requires a building permit, structural engineering drawings, and zoning review for height and setbacks. In some Highland Creek properties, particularly those in or near heritage districts, additional heritage review may be required. The City of Toronto permit process for second-storey additions typically takes 6–12 weeks from complete submission. We manage the entire process.

Can my Highland Creek home support a second storey addition?

Most Highland Creek bungalows can support a second storey addition, but a structural assessment is required to confirm. We evaluate the existing foundation, exterior walls, and floor joist system before finalizing the design. In some cases, beam and post reinforcement is required. We conduct this assessment as part of our pre-construction process at no obligation.
