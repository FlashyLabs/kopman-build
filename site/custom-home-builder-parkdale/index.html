Why Parkdale homeowners choose Kopman Build

Parkdale's housing stock is among the most character-rich in Toronto — but its active heritage designation area means that building here requires more than a standard general contractor. Heritage Preservation Services, period-sensitive materials, and tight lot lines on row house sites demand a team that has been through the process before.

Kopman Build brings that experience directly to Parkdale. We've worked on Victorian restoration projects where matching original brick, cornices, and bay window profiles was as important as the structural rebuild behind them — and on modern kitchen and bathroom renovations inside larger homes where the street facade stays intact while the interior is completely reimagined.

As Parkdale continues to attract owners who take the long view on their properties, the gap between a well-executed build and a hasty renovation becomes visible quickly. We work for clients who understand that difference and want a builder who does too.

Common questions about custom home builds in Parkdale

Can you build a custom home on a Parkdale lot that has heritage designation?

Yes. Many Parkdale properties fall within the neighbourhood's active heritage designation area, which governs what changes can be made to facades and street-facing elements. We are experienced navigating Toronto's Heritage Preservation Services process and work with architects familiar with heritage approvals to ensure your project proceeds without unnecessary delays.

What does a full Victorian restoration or custom build cost in Parkdale?

A complete gut renovation or modern rebuild of a Parkdale Victorian row house typically ranges from $350 to $650+ per square foot depending on heritage requirements, the extent of structural work, and finish level. Heritage facade work and matching original materials can add cost. We provide detailed pre-construction budgets so there are no surprises before a commitment is made.

How does heritage designation affect a Parkdale custom home project?

Toronto's heritage designation in Parkdale typically requires preserving or restoring original exterior elements such as brick, cornices, bay windows, and porch details. Interior work is generally not restricted. Our team coordinates directly with Heritage Preservation Services and can advise early in the design phase to avoid scope changes later in permitting.

Is a full interior renovation of a Parkdale Victorian considered a custom home build?

It depends on the scope. A complete gut renovation that rebuilds the interior structure, systems, and finishes while retaining the existing envelope is a category of work we handle regularly in Parkdale. Ground-up demolition and rebuild is also possible on non-heritage-designated lots. We assess each project individually and advise on the most practical path — renovation, addition, or new build.
