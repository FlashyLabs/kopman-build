Custom Home Builder The Beaches

The Beaches is one of Toronto's most active custom home markets — early 20th century cottages and lakefront family homes on tight lots, steps from the boardwalk, where young families are investing in full rebuilds and heritage-sensitive renovations. Kopman Build has delivered projects like these across the city for over 25 years, with the site discipline and design coordination they demand.

Why Beaches families choose Kopman Build

Building or fully rebuilding in The Beaches means working within real constraints: narrow lots with limited staging room, neighbours close on both sides, and a neighbourhood character that the City and the community both take seriously. Getting it right requires experience with exactly this kind of project.

Kopman Build has been managing complex residential builds in Toronto since 1999. Dustin Kopman grew up in construction through his family's firm, and brings that ground-level understanding to every project — he is directly involved from first consultation through handover.

In The Beaches specifically, that means tight-lot logistics planned before a shovel goes in, heritage character coordinated at the design stage, and a communication rhythm that keeps families informed throughout a disruptive process.

Common questions about custom home builds in The Beaches

Can you build a custom home on a tight lot in The Beaches?

Yes — tight lots are the norm in The Beaches, and we plan for them from day one. We coordinate closely with the architect on setbacks, side yard easements, and staging logistics so that narrow frontages and limited access don't create delays or cost overruns during construction.

How do you handle heritage character requirements in The Beaches?

Many homes in The Beaches sit within or adjacent to heritage conservation areas, which means facade treatments, rooflines, and massing must be reviewed against neighbourhood character guidelines. We work with architects and the City of Toronto's planning department early in the design phase to confirm what is permissible before construction drawings are finalised.

What does a full custom home build cost in The Beaches?

Custom home construction in The Beaches typically runs $450 to $800+ per square foot depending on finishes, structural complexity, and site conditions. Tight lots and heritage sensitivities can add cost at the design and permitting stage. We provide a detailed pre-construction budget before any commitment is made.

Do you work with young families doing full-house renovations in The Beaches?

Frequently. The Beaches has an active market of young families buying early 20th century cottages and undertaking full gut renovations or ground-up rebuilds. We manage these projects with a clear scope, fixed-price contract, and weekly reporting so families can plan around the construction timeline.
