Why Riverdale families choose Kopman Build

Riverdale's east-side premium reflects what the neighbourhood offers: ravine access, mature tree canopy, Riverdale Park, and some of the most character-rich Victorian and Edwardian housing stock in Toronto. Building or rebuilding here requires a contractor who understands the specific zoning, heritage, and conservation authority rules that apply — not just general city bylaws.

Many Riverdale lots involve TRCA review for ravine proximity, angular plane and setback restrictions on narrow Victorian footprints, and infill guidelines that balance new construction with the existing streetscape character along the Broadview corridor. We navigate these regularly and without surprises.

The neighbourhood also runs an active kitchen, bathroom, and addition market — many owners take a phased approach, improving a Victorian semi before committing to a full rebuild. Dustin Kopman has been involved in that kind of long-term client relationship since 1999, and that continuity is something we offer every Riverdale project.

Common questions about custom home builds in Riverdale

Can you build a custom home on a Riverdale Victorian or Edwardian lot?

Yes. Riverdale's housing stock is predominantly late Victorian and Edwardian semi-detached and detached homes, many on narrow city lots. We have extensive experience navigating Toronto's zoning bylaws, heritage overlays, and lot-coverage rules specific to the east side — including setback requirements, angular plane restrictions, and infill guidelines that apply along the Broadview corridor.

Are there special permit considerations for building near Riverdale's ravines and parks?

Absolutely. Properties close to the Don Valley ravine system or Riverdale Park are subject to TRCA (Toronto and Region Conservation Authority) review, which adds a layer of approvals beyond the standard Toronto Building Permit. We manage the full approvals process — including TRCA submission and liaison — so nothing falls through the cracks.

How much does a custom home build typically cost in Riverdale?

Riverdale commands an east-side premium given its proximity to the ravine, walkable amenities, and strong resale values. Custom home construction costs in the neighbourhood typically range from $425 to $800+ per square foot depending on finishes, lot conditions, and design complexity. We provide detailed pre-construction budgets before any commitment is made.

Do you handle kitchen, bathroom, and addition work in Riverdale as well as full custom builds?

Yes. Alongside ground-up custom homes, we carry out kitchen renovations, bathroom renovations, and rear or second-storey additions throughout Riverdale and the east side. The neighbourhood has an active market for all three — many owners invest in a phased program that begins with a kitchen or addition before a full rebuild. We can help you plan that sequence.
